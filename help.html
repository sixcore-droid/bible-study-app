How the Study Workstation works

Three parts: the reading plan on the left, the reader in the middle, and the side panel on the right, opened from the buttons down the right edge.

The top bar

Plan
Shows or hides the reading plan (on narrow screens).
Go to a passage
Type any reference, like Mark 13:14 or Psalm 82, and press Go.
Find in the text
Finds a word in the passage on screen and highlights every match. Enter goes to the next match, Shift+Enter back, Esc clears it. Ctrl+F or Cmd+F jumps here.
My desk / Read along
My desk is your full view. Read along shows what a reader would see: the text, public notes and the public panels only.
?
This guide.

A refresh brings you back to where you were: the plan, the day and reading (or the passage you opened, until midnight), the panel you had open, and how far down the page you were.

Reading plan (left)

Reading plan
Pick a plan: The Divine Council Thread, Gospel of Luke, Psalms 150, F260, F260 NT, The Gospels Together, and the BibleProject plans (Paul's Letters, Major Prophets, Minor Prophets).
Matching psalm
Adds a psalm to each day that fits that day's chapter, with the reason it was picked.
Skip genealogies & lists
Leaves out the genealogy and list chapters, using the skip list from your vault.
Days
Tap a day to read it. Its box ticks itself once every reading that day is marked Done. The bar shows how far through the plan you are.

Reader (middle)

Reading tabs
When a day has more than one reading, each gets a tab at the top.
Open in Logos
Opens the passage in the Logos app, in the version you are reading (the LSB for the BSB, which isn't in your Logos library).
Reading / Verse by verse
One button. Switches between paragraphs and one verse per line.
Side by side / One version
One button. Shows two versions in columns. Pick them in the two version menus (LSB, NLT, ESV, NET, BSB, CSB, KJV).
A− and A+
Text size.
Copy for listening
Copies the passage as plain text, verse numbers in [brackets], ready to paste into Speechify.
Verse numbers
Tap one to open that verse in Verse tools.
Dotted words
Tap one to open it in Word Study.
Done and Next
Done marks this reading finished. Next goes to the next reading.
My notes
Your notes on this passage. Tick Public to let readers see them. Save note keeps them; once saved, Open in Obsidian goes to the note in your vault.

Side panel (right edge buttons)

Tap a button to open its panel; tap it again to close. A number on a button means it has something for this passage. Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider.

Verse
Verse tools: one verse in Hebrew or Greek word order with Strong's numbers, Dead Sea Scrolls readings, and any words in your glossary.
Inter
Interleaved Bible: the passage with the interlinear under each line, and where it sits in the book's outline.
Words
Word Study: every key Hebrew or Greek word in the passage. Tap one for its meaning and where else it is used. "+ Vocab" saves it to your deck.
Council
Divine Council Index: what the Index says about this passage.
Gods
Gods of the Bible: the gods and spiritual beings named here.
Fact Book
People and places in the passage.
Posts
Your jayms.com posts that cite this passage, most citations first.
Library
Corpus Library: texts outside the Bible that bear on this passage (Septuagint, Dead Sea Scrolls, Enoch and more).
Fathers
What the early Church Fathers wrote on each verse, oldest first: an excerpt each, and Read it opens the whole comment on historicalchristian.faith.
Targum
The Aramaic Targums in English, verse by verse under your reading version: Onkelos and Pseudo-Jonathan on the Torah, Targum Jonathan on the Prophets. Complete for the Torah, 1 and 2 Samuel and Isaiah; partial elsewhere; none in English for the Writings.
Cross-refs
Other passages that quote or echo this one, strongest first. Tap one to read it in place; Open puts it in the reader.
BP
BibleProject: Listen (LSB audio), one button per chapter in the reading; playing one carries on through the rest. Then the BibleProject guide to the book, then their videos and articles on this chapter. Podcasts have their own tab.
On X
Draft a post about the passage, with a jayms.com link, and open it in X to post.
Claude
Ask Claude about the passage. It starts from your own material: your notes, the Index, your posts, your vault and past chats. (Works on the claude.ai link only.)
Logos
Logos queue: searches to run in Logos. Copy them, run them, tick them off.
Shelf
Your Logos books for this book of the Bible, in tabs: Commentaries, Study Bibles (Logos plus the open Tyndale and Biblica notes), Maps, and Others (DDD, your courses on this book, and your main authors' books). New Logos purchases are added every morning.
Vocab
Your vocabulary deck.
Ideas
Post ideas you have logged.
Obsidian
Notes in your vault that mention this passage.
Wrap up
Makes the day's four outputs from your notes and saved Claude answers: mind dump, student sheet, quiz and vocabulary. (claude.ai link only.)
Review
Vocabulary cards that are due, and quizzes from past days.
Config
Shelf settings: rank your authors, and order or hide commentaries and study Bibles.
Notes
All of today's notes as one page.
