Why this exists

There are a lot of free Bible study tools out there, and they are scattered across the web. This is an attempt to build something easy to use and intuitive: one stop for those free tools, working together beside the text.

I made this Bible Study Workstation for how I study the Bible.

Add to Home Screen

On an iPhone or iPad, in Safari:

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up) at the bottom or top of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The app then opens from its own icon, full screen, like any other app.

In Chrome or another browser on an iPhone, open this page in Safari first; Apple only allows adding to the Home Screen from Safari.

On Android, in Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Keep a copy of your key

Keep a copy of your Speechify key somewhere safe, such as a password manager or your Speechify account page. If this browser's data is cleared, the key is lost and you will need to paste it again.

Read aloud (Speechify)

Paste your own Speechify API key (from platform.speechify.ai) to turn on reading aloud. The Voice and Read buttons then appear on the text. The key stays in this browser and goes only to Speechify. Treat it like a password for your own account. Speechify's free plan covers about 40 chapters a month.
Voice and speed Once a key is saved, you choose the voice that reads and how fast it reads here. 1x is normal speed; 2x is twice as fast.

Your folder

Keep your notes, settings and progress in a folder on your own computer, so clearing the browser never loses them. The folder is yours: nothing is uploaded and nobody else can see it.
